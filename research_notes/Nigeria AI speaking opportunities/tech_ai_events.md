# Nigerian Tech and AI Events, Summits, Meetups and Communities (late 2026 to 2027) for Beginner-AI Talks and Workshops

Research date: 2026-10-21. Method note: the findings below come mostly from web-search result summaries. Direct fetches of sessionize.com and techeconomy.ng failed (DNS errors in the research environment), so CFP windows and dates quoted from Sessionize and Techeconomy have NOT been checked against the live page. Treat every date as "verify before acting". Items marked **[UNVERIFIED]** rest on one aggregator or one snippet. No private personal contact data is included; contact routes are organisational sites and pages only.

## Q1. Which major Nigerian tech/AI events run recurring editions, and when?

### Takeaway
The recurring calendar has three parts. (1) Large flagship expos (GITEX Nigeria, Aug/Sep; Lagos Tech Fest, Feb; Lagos Startup Week, July). (2) An AI-policy and research tier (DSN AI Bootcamp, late Oct; Nigeria AI Collective "Build", Oct, Abuja; DataFest Africa). (3) A dense GDG DevFest season (Oct to Dec) across about 10 or more Nigerian cities, plus I/O Extended (Jul/Aug). For late 2026, the only realistic speaking slots are the DevFests still to come, and most of their CFPs have already closed. The main planning horizon is the 2027 cycle.

## Q2. Which have open speaker calls (Sessionize, Google Forms, Papercall) and when do they typically open and close?

### Takeaway
GDG DevFests are the only consistently public, open CFP channel (Sessionize). They typically open May to August and close July to early October for Oct to Dec events, and speaking is free (a stipend is not guaranteed). The big commercial expos and AI summits show no public CFP. PyCon Nigeria and DataFest Africa have historically used public CFPs (DataFest: about May to June).

## Q3. Which community groups host recurring meetups that welcome guest facilitators?

### Takeaway
GDG chapters (Lagos, Ajah, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Cloud PH, Enugu, Abeokuta, Akure, Gombe, Kafanchan, Ogbomoso, Warri, Lokoja, Dutse, Lafia) run year-round events: Build with AI, I/O Extended, Women Techmakers/IWD, and Study Jams. They are the most accessible entry point. AI Saturdays Lagos, DSN, She Code Africa and Data Community Africa are learning-oriented communities where a beginner-AI workshop fits naturally.

It offers free weekend study groups for beginners and early-career professionals, run as 16-Saturday cohorts. — [TechCabal 2019](https://techcabal.com/2019/10/04/my-life-in-tech-tejumade-afonja-is-running-the-biggest-ai-classes-you-havent-heard-of/); [AI4D](https://www.ai4d.ai/blog/tejumade-afonja-ai-saturdays-lagos-on-ai-and-local-communities). Current activity status in 2026 is **[UNVERIFIED]**. The sources date from 2019.

Examples include "AI for Business & Productivity (ChatGPT, LLM tools, automations)", "GenAI for Africa", "AI Growth Lab: Leveraging AI for Revenue Growth", "Unlocking AI Potential: Microsoft Copilot", "AgentCon 2025 Lagos", and "AI in Action – Turning Ideas to Reality Lagos Meetup". These show demand for business-oriented, non-technical AI sessions. — [createwith.com Nigeria](https://www.createwith.com/countries/nigeria); [AI for Business](https://www.createwith.com/event/lagos-ai-for-business-productivity-chatgpt-llm-tools-automations-c-nov-2025); [AgentCon Lagos](https://www.createwith.com/event/lagos-agentcon-2025-lagos-may-2025)

- She Code Africa runs a community and a Lagos summit (500+ attendees in 2026). — [Zikoko](https://www.zikoko.com/announcements/a-decade-of-impact-she-code-africa-to-convene-500-african-women-in-tech-in-lagos/)

### Inferences
- Pitching a free 60 to 90 minute "AI for Beginners" workshop to GDG chapter organisers (via each chapter page's contact on gdg.community.dev) is likely the fastest route to stage time. The best timing is outside DevFest season (Jan to Jun) and around Women Techmakers/IWD in March. ### Gaps
- No published facilitator-application processes were found for AI Saturdays Lagos, She Code Africa, or DSN.
- No official email or social handles were captured for individual communities. Use the official websites: datasciencenigeria.org, shecodeafrica.org (inferred domain), saturdays.ai, gdg.community.dev chapter pages.

## Q4. What is the realistic audience, and which events suit a beginner or non-technical AI talk? (Fit ratings)

DevFests and PyCon are developer-heavy. They are easy to get on stage at, but their prospecting value for a MERL consultant is mostly low to medium.
