Fit rating scale (for a MERL/development consultant doing beginner AI talks): **High** means there is a documented train-the-trainer or partner-delivery model, or a beginner/public-sector audience, plus a plausible entry route. **Medium** means the audience is relevant but the entry route is unclear or competitive. **Low** means the audience is advanced/technical or there is no visible route for an external facilitator.

## Federal and state government programmes

### Takeaway
The strongest government entry points are partner and train-the-trainer channels, not open "speaker calls." I found no open 2026 call for external AI trainers from NITDA or 3MTT. The live channels are these:
- NITDA's National Digital Literacy Dashboard, which asks hubs, NGOs and institutions to register.
- The 3MTT Partner Network, with a category for training and delivery partners.
- The UNESCO/EU-funded civil-service AI literacy workshops (federal, Enugu, Cross River).
- AI master-trainer programmes (ECOWAS Small Business Coalition/INNOPOWER with NITDA; EkoAI Academy with LSETF/Lagos Innovates).

Public-service AI literacy fits a MERL/development profile best.

### Inferences
- Government channels pay for delivery through funded partners (3MTT training providers, UNESCO/EU projects, InnoPower master-trainer schemes) rather than buying one-off talks. The consultant's best play is to position as a delivery partner or master trainer, or as an M&E partner for these cascades. The cascade models (DL4ALL, EkoAI, ECOWAS SBC) clearly need learning-outcome monitoring.
- State 3MTT Learning Community meetups are the fastest way to get visible, unpaid beginner-AI speaking slots.

### Gaps
- No 2026–27 open call for external AI trainers or speakers was found for NITDA, NCAIR or 3MTT. The NITDA Digital Nigeria conference/exhibition, NCC events and Galaxy Backbone events returned nothing in the searches run.

## Innovation hubs and corporate tech programmes

### Takeaway
Hub-run programming in 2026 is dominated by large funded skilling programmes: CcHUB GATEWAY (Mastercard Foundation), Microsoft's AI National Skilling Initiative via TeKnowledge, and Meta AI Academy Nigeria. These use delivery partners and facilitators.
