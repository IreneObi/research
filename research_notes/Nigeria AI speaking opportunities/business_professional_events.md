# Nigerian Business, SME, Professional-Association and Women/Youth-in-Business Events for Beginner "AI for Business" Talks (Oct 2026 to 2027)

Research date: 2026-10-21. Method: web search (about 17 calls). Direct page fetches of lagosinternationaltradefair.com and cipmnigeria.org failed (DNS errors in the research sandbox), so details come from search-indexed snippets and press coverage. Treat dates for 2027 as **inferred from previous editions** unless marked confirmed. Contact emails were **not verified** for any organization. Check the official sites before outreach.

## Chambers and employer bodies (LCCI/LITF, ACCI, NACCIMA, NECA, MAN, NESG)

### Takeaway
Two big confirmed events are still ahead in 2026: NESG's NES#32 (Abuja, 26-27 Oct 2026) and LCCI's 40th Lagos International Trade Fair (Lagos, 6-15 Nov 2026). Both are too close or too high-level to get a speaking slot cold. The realistic routes are LITF side seminars or exhibitor and B2B sessions, plus pitching for the 2027 cycles: ACCI's Abuja trade fair (around late Sept 2027) and NECA's summit, which becomes an "International Employers' Summit" from 2027 (around June 2027).

### Cited Findings
- **LCCI – 40th Lagos International Trade Fair (LITF):** confirmed for 6-15 Nov 2026 at Tafawa Balewa Square, Lagos. Organisers expect more than 1,000 exhibitors and over 500,000 visitors from 20 countries. Entry is free, and there is a dedicated B2B/B2G room. Official site: lagosinternationaltradefair.com. LCCI has warned about fraudulent events using the LITF name — [Rio Times](https://www.riotimesonline.com/lagos-international-trade-fair-2026/); [Guardian](https://guardian.ng/?p=2925686); [Shipping Position](https://shippingposition.com.ng/lcci-targets-1m-visitors-expanded-trade-fair/)
- **NESG – Nigerian Economic Summit NES#32:** 26-27 Oct 2026, Transcorp Hilton Abuja, with a virtual option. Theme: "Growth that Works: Delivering Jobs, Productivity and Shared Prosperity". Voice of Nigeria (VON) gives 25-27 Oct, which conflicts with other sources — [NESG agenda](https://www.nesgroup.org/32/agenda); [NAN](https://nannews.ng/fg-inaugurates-32nd-nes-committee/); [VON](https://von.gov.ng/nigeria-partners-nesg-to-boost-economic-diplomacy-investment/)
- **ACCI – 21st Abuja International Trade Fair:** 25 Sept-6 Oct 2026 (one source says it started 26 Sept), so it has already passed. Theme: "Resilience: Trade, Taxation and the Economy". ACCI projected 500+ exhibitors and around 100,000 visitors. Focus sectors included fintech, digital payments and tax technology — [NAN](https://nannews.ng/2026-abuja-trade-fair-targets-100000-visitors-showcases-10000-products/); [MSME Africa](https://msmeafricaonline.com/abuja-trade-fair-to-connect-500-exhibitors-with-100000-visitors-in-2026/)
- **NECA – Nigeria Employers' Summit 2026 (5th edition):** held 29-30 June 2026 at Abuja Continental Hotel (passed). Theme: "Leveraging Reforms and ESG for Enterprise Competitiveness…". NECA says the summit becomes the International Employers' Summit from 2027 — [ThisDay](https://thisdaylive.com/2026/06/25/neca-to-lead-conversation-on-impact-of-economic-reforms); [VON](https://von.gov.ng/neca-upgrades-employers-summit-to-international-event/)
- **NECA Workforce Job and Employability Fair:** 13-14 May 2026, NECA House, Ikeja (passed). It may repeat in 2027 (unverified) — [Leadership](https://leadership.ng/neca-to-host-2026-workforce-job-employability-fair/)
- NECA press releases are published at news.neca.org.ng — [NECA news](https://news.neca.org.ng/press-releases/)

### Inferences
- **LITF fit: Medium.** The audience is mass-market traders and SMEs, not managers. The best angle is a free "AI for small business" clinic at an exhibitor stand or a sponsored side seminar. The likely route is sponsorship or exhibiting rather than a call for papers, but this is unverified.
- **NES fit: Low** for a beginner consultant. Speakers are invited, high-level policy figures. Attending to network is still valuable. NES#33 is expected around Oct 2027 (inferred).
- **ACCI AITF 2027 fit: Medium.** Expected around late Sept-early Oct 2027 (inferred). Pitch ACCI's trade fair secretariat around Q2 2027 for a seminar or "SME day" slot. Its fintech and tax-tech themes align with AI.
- **NECA International Employers' Summit 2027 fit: Medium-High.** Expected around June 2027 (inferred). The audience is employers and HR/management, who are buyers of AI upskilling. Approach NECA's training/secretariat department early in 2027.

### Gaps
- No 2026-27 data found for NACCIMA or MAN (Manufacturers Association of Nigeria) events, such as the MAN AGM, which is usually mid-year. Not verified.
- Official LCCI and LITF programme/seminar contacts, and LITF special-day seminar formats, could not be fetched (site unreachable from the sandbox).
- No pricing found for speaking, sponsorship or exhibit packages at any of these events.

## Professional bodies with CPD/MCPD programmes (CIPM, NIM, ICAN, CIBN, NIPR, APCON, NSE, CIoD, CIS, NBA)

### Takeaway
Most flagship annual conferences for 2026 have already happened: CIPM (Sept), CIBN (Sept), NBA AGC (Aug) and ICAN's conference (ends 23 Oct). The real opportunity is the **2027 cycle and the year-round CPD, zonal and chapter programmes**. NIM explicitly runs AI capacity-building at zonal and chapter level. ICAN's president is publicly pushing AI skills. The NBA's 2026 AGC had 27 breakout tracks and 200+ speakers. These bodies are the strongest pitch targets for paid beginner AI workshops.

### Cited Findings
- **CIPM (HR) – 58th International Conference & Exhibition:** 21-24 Sept 2026, Abuja (passed). Theme: "Repositioning for Value and Impact". Topics included workforce transformation and digitalisation. CIPM bills it as "Africa's largest HR event" — [CIPM](https://cipmnigeria.org/cipm-announces-theme-dates-and-opens-registration-for-the-58th-international-conference-and-exhibition-africas-largest-hr-event/)
- **NIM (Nigerian Institute of Management):** the president says NIM runs "capacity-building programmes at zonal and chapter levels to bring our members up to speed… on the implications of AI". NIM publishes a 2026 Management, Leadership and Governance Courses (MLGC) training calendar of instructor-led virtual courses — [BusinessDay](https://businessday.ng/?p=1102979); [NIM registration page](https://nim.ng/?p=1430); [NAN](https://nannews.ng/65th-anniversary-nim-president-calls-for-improved-governance-institutional-management/)
- **ICAN – 56th Annual Accountants Conference:** 18-23 Oct 2026, Abuja (in progress now). It is described as Africa's largest gathering of accounting professionals — [NAN](https://nannews.ng/?p=343807). The ICAN president says accountants will adopt AI tools while keeping professional ethics — [Leadership](https://leadership.ng/accountants-will-adopt-ai-tools-but-keep-professional-ethics-ican-president/)
- **ANAN (accountants) MCPD:** 12-14 Oct 2026, Yola, with 2,000+ participants (passed). ANAN's model is a zonal MCPD series with fees and registration on its website — [NAN](https://nannews.ng/?p=343807); [ANAN 2024 MCPD programme PDF](https://anan.org.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/2024-MCPD-Programme-UPDATED.pdf)
- **CIBN – 19th Annual Banking & Finance Conference:** 8-9 Sept 2026, Transcorp Hilton Abuja (passed). Organisers advertised 30+ speakers and 10,000+ expected attendees. A Consultative Committee is chaired by Wema Bank's CEO — [CIBN ABF site](https://abfconference.cibng.org/); [ThisDay](https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/08/28/wema-bank-ceo-oseni-to-chair-cibn-19th-annual-banking-finance-conference/)
- **NBA (Nigerian Bar Association) – 66th AGC "Beyond Limits":** 21-28 Aug 2026, Port Harcourt (passed). It had 45+ sessions, 27 breakout tracks and 200+ speakers, and is run by an AGC Planning Committee. Its blog is at blog.nigerianbar.org.ng — [NBA programme PDF](https://blog.nigerianbar.org.ng/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/AGC2026_Beyond_Limits_Programme-1.pdf); [NBA blog](https://blog.nigerianbar.org.ng/?p=9653). The NBA Women Forum held a separate conference in Kano, 27 Mar-1 Apr 2026 — [NBA blog](https://blog.nigerianbar.org.ng/?p=8506)
- **Chartered Institute of Directors (CIoD) Nigeria (formerly IoD):** its 2026 Annual Directors' Conference will be in Lagos; no dates found. The 2025 edition was 22-23 Oct 2025 in Abuja. The Women Directors' Biennial Conference was held 1 July 2026 in Lagos — [Guardian](https://guardian.ng/?p=2867531); [Leadership](https://leadership.ng/directors-institute-targets-greater-female-influence-in-corporate-decision-making/)
- **2026 Annual Corporate Governance Conference** (organiser at corpgovnigeria.org, not CIoD): 8 Oct 2026, Victoria Island, Lagos (passed) — [corpgovnigeria.org](https://corpgovnigeria.org/2026-annual-corporate-governance-conference/)

### Inferences
- **NIM: High fit.** It already runs AI awareness programmes at chapter level, so pitch a beginner "AI for managers" module to NIM chapters or zones as an external facilitator. A **paid facilitator engagement** is likely (not verified).
- **CIPM 2027 conference: High fit.** Expected around Sept 2027 (inferred from the Sept 2026 edition). CIPM also runs year-round training and chapter events. An HR audience buys AI upskilling.
- **ICAN and ANAN: High fit** through district societies and MCPD sessions. ICAN's 2027 conference is expected around Oct 2027 (inferred). District-society monthly meetings are an easy entry point (unverified).
- **CIBN 2027: Medium.** Expected around Sept 2027 (inferred). Speakers appear to be invited through the committee and sponsors. CIBN's branch CPD events are an easier route.
- **NBA AGC 2027: Medium-High.** Expected around late Aug 2027 (inferred). With 27 breakout tracks, the Section on Business Law and tech-law sessions need "AI for lawyers" content. The 2027 venue is not known.
- **CIoD Annual Directors' Conference:** board-level audience, high value but invite-only (inferred). Low-Medium fit for a beginner talk; good for networking.

### Gaps
- No 2026-27 information found for NIPR, APCON, NSE (Nigerian Society of Engineers) or the Chartered Institute of Stockbrokers. These were not searched individually due to the tool budget.
- None of the bodies publishes a formal speaker call for papers that I found. The selection mechanism (planning committee or invitation) is inferred.
- Facilitator fees and CPD-provider accreditation requirements are not found.
- CIoD 2026 Annual Directors' Conference dates are not found. If it follows the 2025 pattern (late Oct), it may be imminent.

## SME platforms, banks, foundations, women/youth networks and business schools

### Takeaway
The best-fit near-term targets are **WIMBIZ's 25th Annual Conference** (5-6 Nov 2026, Eko Hotels, Lagos, run with the EDC at Pan-Atlantic University) and **Access Bank's W Initiative Womenpreneur Pitch-a-Ton season 8**, whose three-week mini-MBA starts Nov 2026. Both convene women SME owners and run training content. EDC Pan-Atlantic runs several sponsor-funded SME capacity programmes (with Providus Bank and SMEDAN/AGRA) that use training tracks. SMEDAN has capacity building as a 2026 pillar but no AI-specific event was found.

### Cited Findings
- **WIMBIZ 25th Annual Conference:** 5-6 Nov 2026, Eko Hotels & Suites, Lagos. It runs in partnership with EDC Pan-Atlantic and includes one-on-one business consultations ("The Fix Room"). A press conference marking the 25th anniversary was held 13 Oct 2026. Source is a LinkedIn post plus press, so treat as partly verified — [WIMBIZ LinkedIn](https://ng.linkedin.com/in/wimbiz-ng); [BellaNaija](https://www.bellanaija.com/?p=1787858); [Guardian (past editions held in November)](https://guardian.ng/?p=1051088)
- **Access Bank W Initiative – Womenpreneur Pitch-a-Ton season 8:** targets 120 women business owners. The three-week mini-MBA starts Nov 2026 and is run with the IFC. Over seven seasons, 1,141+ women were trained across 10 countries — [MSME Africa](https://msmeafricaonline.com/access-bank-launches-8th-womenpreneur-programme-to-train-120-women-support-business-growth/)
- **EDC Pan-Atlantic University:** ran an AG-SME Bootcamp with SMEDAN, AGRA and Kaduna Business School in Apr 2026 — [TechEconomy](https://techeconomy.ng/edc-partners-empower-agric-smes-with-training-n1m-grants). It also delivers the 3-month capacity track for the Providus Bank SME Program 2026 (applications 16 Apr-31 May 2026; for businesses 5+ years old with ₦50m+ turnover) — [Opportunities for Africans](https://www.opportunitiesforafricans.com/providus-bank-sme-program-2026-cohort-6-for-young-nigerian-entrepreneurs/)
- **SMEDAN:** its 2026 roadmap includes capacity building (expanding entrepreneurship and business-management training). SMEDAN and FIRS run a weekly MSME Business Clinic, Fridays 10am-1pm — [BusinessDay](https://businessday.ng/news/article/smedan-targets-msmes-formalisation-funding-expansion-in-2026/); [VON](https://von.gov.ng/smedan-unveils-2026-roadmap-for-msme-growth/)
- **Tony Elumelu Foundation:** announced a 2026 cohort of 3,200 entrepreneurs on 22 Mar 2026. No 2026 TEF Forum date was found. Past forums were held in Lagos in October — [TEF](https://www.tonyelumelufoundation.org/?p=28486); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Tony_Elumelu_Foundation)
- **She Leads Africa:** the 2026 BoostHer Program (with Jobberman) for young Nigerian women entrepreneurs includes a Lagos career and trade fair with digital-skills sessions — [Opportunity Desk](https://opportunitydesk.org/2026/08/25/sla-boosther-program-2026/)
- **African Workforce Summit 2026 (Lagos):** future-of-work and HR event, with 5,000+ expected attendees and career workshops. Date not found (unverified listing) — [Selar listing](https://selar.com/l3o542gc28)
- Fidelity Bank has run a women-SME Apprenticeship Support Initiative. The snippet is undated and possibly old — [MSME Africa](https://msmeafricaonline.com/?p=55469)

### Inferences
- **WIMBIZ: High fit.** The audience is senior women professionals and business owners. The 2026 programme is probably fixed already, so pitch to volunteer for "The Fix Room" consultations or for 2027 programming and WIMBIZ chapter events (inferred).
- **Access Bank W / Pitch-a-Ton: High fit.** A guest "AI for your business" session in the mini-MBA is plausible. The route is the W Initiative team; selection is likely by invitation or partnership (inferred).
- **EDC Pan-Atlantic: High fit.** It runs many sponsor-funded SME bootcamps and is a likely buyer of external facilitators. It is also a gateway to WIMBIZ, SMEDAN and bank programmes.
- **SMEDAN: Medium fit.** Government route that needs a partnership proposal. AI training is not yet visible in its programming, which is a gap the consultant could fill.
- **TEF: Medium fit.** A large youth-entrepreneur audience, but they are mostly early-stage (lower purchasing power). TEFConnect and webinar content are possible routes.

### Gaps
- No 2026-27 findings for FirstBank SME, Sterling, Zenith SME events, Fidelity SME Hub, Ventures Platform (Abuja), Women in Business Nigeria, or Lagos Business School and alumni events. These were not searched due to the tool budget.
- WIMBIZ ticket prices and programme contacts are not found.
