# Nigerian Development, NGO/CSO and MERL Events (Oct 2026 – 2027): Speaking Opportunities for Beginner "AI for M&E" Content

Research date: 2026-10-21. Method: web search (search-engine summaries) plus one attempted fetch of nnngo.org, which failed with a DNS error. Many findings come from search-result snippets and LinkedIn posts, not from organizers' official pages. Items like these are marked **[UNVERIFIED]**, and inferred dates are marked **[INFERRED]**. ## Q1: Nigerian evaluation bodies and conferences (NAE, gLOCAL, AfrEA, evidence events)

### Takeaway
The most valuable near-term target is the Nigeria Evaluation Conference (NAE2026), 2–4 Nov 2026 in Abuja. Its theme strand on "emerging trends and innovation" fits AI for M&E, but its abstract window has almost certainly closed, so the realistic goal is attending and networking now and submitting for the 2027 edition. gLOCAL Evaluation Week (June each year, free, self-organized sessions) is the easiest place to host your own beginner AI-for-M&E session in Nigeria. AfrEA 2026 is in Morocco, not Nigeria.

### Cited Findings
- **Nigeria Evaluation Conference 2026 (#NAE2026), likely run by the Nigerian Association of Evaluators.** 2–4 November 2026 in Abuja. Theme: "Evaluation as a National Asset." Strands: evaluation education, national evaluation systems and policy uptake, and emerging trends and innovation. The call for abstracts was shared through a LinkedIn repost, and the concept note and guidelines were sent by email. **[UNVERIFIED: no official NAE webpage found; source is a LinkedIn post surfaced in search]** — [LinkedIn (Seember Ishuh profile/post)](https://ng.linkedin.com/in/seemberishuh)
- NAE is the umbrella body for M&E associations in Nigeria (2019 government report) — [FMINO / RIO Center](https://fmino.gov.ng/monitoring-evaluation-practitioners-get-professional-association/)
- **gLOCAL Evaluation Week 2026** ran 1–5 June 2026. It was hybrid, themed "Evaluation, Evidence, and Trust in the Age of AI." Sessions are organized by volunteers and are free to attend — [BetterEvaluation listing](https://www.betterevaluation.org/community/events/glocal-evaluation-week-2026); [Global Evaluation Initiative gLOCAL page](https://www.globalevaluationinitiative.org/glocal-home)
- In gLOCAL 2024, Anglophone Africa organized 40 events across Ghana, Kenya, Nigeria, South Africa, Tanzania and Uganda — [glocalevalweek.org](https://glocalevalweek.org/node/64214)
- **12th AfrEA International Conference:** Marrakech, Morocco, 23–27 Nov 2026. Theme on evaluation in a polycrisis, sovereignty and resilience. Not Nigeria-hosted. **[UNVERIFIED: from a presenter's LinkedIn post]** — [LinkedIn (Ayokunle Omoniyi)](https://ng.linkedin.com/in/ayokunle-omoniyi)

Its theme was "Technology and Innovation in Evaluation Practice in Africa," and AI was discussed — [GEI reflections](https://globalevaluationinitiative.org/node/63631); [BetterEvaluation](https://www.betterevaluation.org/community/events/11th-afrea-international-conference)
- **Africa Evidence Summit 2026** (CEGA/NIERA) was held in Addis Ababa, 23–24 July 2026, themed "Powering Policy with Innovative, Inclusive, Transparent Data." Not in Nigeria — [CEGA](https://cega.berkeley.edu/event/2026-africa-evidence-summit/)

- **AEAA 2027 (Association for Educational Assessment in Africa):** NECO will host it in Abuja, 16–20 Aug 2027. Education assessment audience — [ThisDay](https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/08/22/nigerias-educational-assessment-receives-boost-to-host-2027-aeaa/); [VON](https://von.gov.ng/nigeria-to-host-2027-africa-educational-assessment-conference/)

### Inferences
- NAE2026: the abstract deadline has very likely passed, since the conference is less than two weeks away. Attending is still high value for prospecting (evaluators, government M&E units, donor projects). Fit for beginner AI content: **High**. Prospecting value: **High**. Watch for an NAE2027 call, which is likely mid-2027 **[INFERRED]**.
- gLOCAL 2027 is likely in early June 2027, with a call for session proposals around Feb–Apr 2027 **[INFERRED from the 2026 pattern]**. It costs nothing and you can self-host a Nigeria session (virtual or in Abuja/Lagos). Fit: **High**, and the 2026 theme was AI. Prospecting value: **Medium–High**.
- NFPC (biennial-style) and AEAA 2027 are sector conferences where an "AI for programme M&E" abstract could fit next cycle. Fit: **Medium**.
- AfrEA's next conference after 2026 is probably around 2028 (roughly two-year cadence) **[INFERRED]**. It is not Nigeria-hosted, so it is low priority under the constraints.

## Q2: NGO/CSO, health, development-partner, philanthropy and economic-summit events: who accepts speakers, and when?

### Takeaway
Nigeria Health Watch's Future of Health Conference (29 Oct 2026, Abuja) and NESG's NES#32 (26–27 Oct 2026, Abuja) are imminent and invitation-curated, with no open speaker call found. They are best used to attend and network, and to pitch for 2027. NNNGO runs an annual conference (November, Lagos in 2025) and has had a "call for facilitators" page, which makes it the most promising direct route for a guest-facilitated AI capacity session for NGOs.

## Q3: Networks running webinars or learning series, and AI or data-for-development events in Nigeria

### Takeaway
Youth and peer evaluation networks (YEE Nigeria / EvalYouth Nigeria, NGYouthSDGs) run forums and M&E trainings and are low-barrier venues for guest facilitators. Data Science Nigeria is the main AI-capacity ecosystem player, with an AI Bootcamp from 26–31 Oct 2026. No Open Data Day Nigeria 2027 listing was found yet; Open Data Day usually falls in early March.

### Cited Findings
- **YEE Nigeria** (describes itself as "EvalYouth in Nigeria") held a 2026 Youth in Evaluation Forum with a panel on power dynamics in evaluation. The date and format are not stated — [YEE Nigeria LinkedIn](https://ng.linkedin.com/in/yee-nigeria); [Seember Ishuh post](https://ng.linkedin.com/in/seemberishuh)
- **NGYouthSDGs** (Nigerian Youth SDGs Network) was the Eval4Action 2025 Emerging Champion and ran a two-day M&E training for champions — [NGYouthSDGs](https://nigerianyouthsdgs.org/tag/monitoring-and-evaluation/); [training post](https://nigerianyouthsdgs.org/ngyouthsdgs-organizes-2-days-monitoring-and-evaluation-training-for-champions-across-nigeria/)
- **Data Science Nigeria (DSN) AI Bootcamp 2026:** hybrid, 26–31 Oct 2026, free (all expenses paid) for selected participants, including the top 400 from the qualifying hackathon. Marks DSN's 10th anniversary — [Data Science Nigeria](https://datasciencenigeria.org/?p=19124)
- Other Nigerian AI events (mostly business/tech audiences, not development): Bluechip Data & AI Summit, Lagos, 10 June 2026 (3rd edition) — [Nairametrics](https://nairametrics.com/2026/03/13/bluechip-data-ai-summit-returns-for-the-3rd-edition-on-june-10-2026/); AI event listings for Nigeria — [createwith.com](https://www.createwith.com/countries/nigeria)
- DataFest Africa (Lagos) hosted a Nigeria AI Collective session in its 2025 edition — [TechEconomy](https://techeconomy.ng/nigeria-ai-collective-leads-the-charge-for-homegrown-ai-innovation-at-datafest-africa-2025)

### Inferences
- YEE Nigeria / EvalYouth and NGYouthSDGs are good for webinars that build visibility. Fit: **High** (beginner audience). Client-prospecting value: **Low–Medium** (young evaluators rather than budget holders). Contact them through their LinkedIn pages.
- DSN events have a technical audience. They are better for partnerships than for finding M&E clients. Fit: **Low–Medium**.
- DataFest Africa (Lagos; past editions were held in the second half of the year) could fit a "data/AI for development" session **[INFERRED; 2026–27 dates not verified]**.
- Open Data Day 2027 is likely in early March 2027, with self-organized local events in Nigeria possible **[INFERRED from the general Open Data Day pattern; no Nigeria source found]**.

### Gaps
- Not found: Development Gateway, MandE NEWS and Nigerian M&E LinkedIn communities with guest-facilitator calls; Nigeria Data Protection Commission events; a Nigeria "Data for Development" event for 2026–27.
