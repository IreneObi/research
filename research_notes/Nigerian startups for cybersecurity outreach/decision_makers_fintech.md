# Decision Makers & Official Contact Channels: Fintech Outreach Targets (Payments/BaaS + Lending/Wealth/Insurtech/Crypto)

Research date: 2026-10-20. Source lists: `payments_banking_infra.md` and `lending_wealth_insurtech_crypto.md` in this folder.

## Method and rules (read first)

- **Sources.** All evidence comes from WebSearch result snippets and summaries. Direct page fetches to company sites failed in this environment (DNS errors / proxy 403), so every page cited below was seen in search results, not opened. Re-check each page in a browser before outreach.
- **Search budget.** The session's web-search budget (200 calls) ran out before the contact-channel pass was finished. Official contact channels were therefore **not retrieved** for Daya, Trove, Busha, Quidax, Roqqu, Blockradar, Breet, Patricia and Yellow Card. Decision makers for those companies were researched first, so their people rows are complete.
- **LinkedIn.** A LinkedIn URL is listed only if it appeared verbatim in a search result. URLs were never built from names, and "not found" means none surfaced. Only one founder LinkedIn URL surfaced in the whole session: Zone's Obi Emetarom.
- **X/Twitter.** No handle for any listed person was cited in a retrieved source, so every handle is "not found".
- **Privacy.** No personal emails or personal or mobile phone numbers were collected. No email-pattern guessing was done, and no data-broker, "contacts" or leak sites were used (getlatka/contactout pages that appeared in results were ignored). Contact channels are **role/department mailboxes and pages published by the company itself**, such as privacy policies, terms, contact pages and help centres. Two mailboxes named after an individual were seen in company documents and were deliberately left out: one in Curacel's DPA and one in Bamboo's privacy policy. Company phone lines were also left out.
- **Confidence scale (per person).** HIGH means a dated 2025–2026 source names the person in the role. MEDIUM means a credible but undated profile (YC, Crunchbase, CB Insights, speaker bio), or 2025–26 evidence that confirms the person but not the exact title. LOW means only pre-2025 evidence, or a low-reliability source.
- **Excluded (shut down or absorbed):** Okra, Gigbanc, Brass, Chimoney, LazerPay, Baxi and Mono (now part of Flutterwave).
- **Corrections to the source notes:**
  - Raenest's third co-founder/CTO is **Sodruldeen Mustapha**, not "Sodiq Akinjobi".
  - Casava *is* active as of Aug 2025, when it appointed a new COO.
  - Juicyway co-founder and COO Justin Ziegler has **left**.
  - Paystack co-founder and CTO Ezra Olubi **exited in 2025**.
