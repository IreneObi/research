# Decision makers and official contact channels: health, edtech, logistics/mobility/commerce, agri/energy/SaaS/other

Research date: 2026-10-20. Covers every company in `health_edtech.md`, `logistics_mobility_commerce.md` and `agri_energy_saas_other.md` except the ones those notes exclude (54gene, Healthlane, Medsaf, Edukoya, Gokada, GoLemon, Heroshe, Joovlin, Hytch, Bento Africa, Sabi, Kobo360, Okra).

## Method and limits (read first)

- **Sources.** Everything came from WebSearch result summaries. Direct page fetches failed: WebFetch returned DNS errors and the proxy refused curl. Every claim cites the URL that the search returned.
- **Search budget.** The shared limit of 200 web searches per turn ran out partway through the contact-channel searches. Official contact channels were therefore **not searched** for most logistics, agri, energy and SaaS companies. Those cells say "not searched (budget exhausted)", and the decision-maker data for those companies is still complete. A follow-up session could fill these gaps with domain-restricted searches, for example `allowed_domains: ["treepz.com"]` and the query "contact email privacy".
- **LinkedIn.** A personal LinkedIn URL is listed only where that exact URL appeared in a search result or in the existing notes. The match was made by name, so check that the profile belongs to the right person before using it. **No URLs were built from names.** Everyone else is marked "not found".
- **X/Twitter.** No handles were cited in any source found, so every person is marked "not found".
- **Privacy.** No personal emails, personal or mobile phone numbers, email-pattern guesses or data-broker sources were used. Some company pages publish addresses tied to named staff, such as founder-name partnership addresses or a named DPO's phone number. These were **left out on purpose**, and only the page URL is given. Company switchboard and WhatsApp numbers were also left out, because Nigerian business lines are often mobile numbers.
- **Confidence levels.**
  - **High:** a 2025–2026 dated source names the person in the role.
  - **Medium:** the source is undated or from 2023–2024, or is a directory or company page, and no report of a change was found.
  - **Low:** the sources conflict, are thin, or there are signs of a change.
