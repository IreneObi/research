## Q2. Which have documented security/fraud incidents or regulatory pressure that create a pitch angle?

### Takeaway
Documented incidents/sanctions cluster at the large players (Flutterwave breaches 2023–2024; CBN onboarding ban on Opay, Moniepoint, Kuda, PalmPay, Paga in Apr 2024; reported CBN fines on Moniepoint and OPay). For the smaller targets, no named breach or sanction was found; the realistic pitch angle is sector-wide pressure: NDPC "full enforcement mode" in 2026, EFCC naming fintechs over weak KYC, CBN supervisory pilots, and rising digital fraud.

### Cited Findings
- Flutterwave: insiders said ₦11B (~$7M) illegally moved in April 2024 (another insider: ≥₦20B), split into small transfers to dodge fraud thresholds; Flutterwave confirmed "unauthorized activities ... on one of our platforms" but said no customer funds/data compromised and disputed the ₦11B figure; this was the 4th reported incident in 14 months, including ₦2.9B (Feb 2023) and ₦19B POS-related (Oct 2023) — [Techpoint](https://techpoint.africa/2024/05/16/flutterwaves-11-billion-breach/), [Techloy](https://www.techloy.com/flutterwave-hit-by-n11-billion-cybersecurity-breach-claims-customer-funds-not-affected/), [Techeconomy](https://www.techeconomy.ng/confusion-as-flutterwave-admits-fraud-attack-denies-losing-11-billion)
- CBN ordered OPay, Moniepoint, Kuda, PalmPay and Paga to stop onboarding new customers on 29 April 2024 (KYC concerns / illegal FX probe; reason disputed); lifted after ~6 weeks–2 months — [Guardian NG](https://guardian.ng/news/cbn-stops-opay-kuda-palmpay-moniepoint-from-boarding-new-customers/), [TechInAfrica](https://www.techinafrica.com/nigerian-central-bank-lifts-ban-on-onboarding-new-customers-for-prominent-fintechs/)
- CBN reportedly fined Moniepoint and OPay ₦1B each after a routine audit (Q2 2024), with at least four other fintechs penalised; OPay denied the figure; amounts not officially disclosed — [TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2024/12/10/cbn-fines-moniepoint-and-opay/); different total (~$633,990) reported by [The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/fraud-and-fincrime/news/cbn-fines-moniepoint-and-opay-usd-633990-in-q2)

### Inferences
- Pitch angle for small firms: "your partner banks / CBN pilot / NDPC audit return will ask for X — I can help you document and evidence it cheaply" (NDPA gap support, policies, email-auth, phishing training). Avoid citing a specific company's incident unless it is public and sourced.
- Juicyway, Daya and Zone (crypto/stablecoin exposure) face the sharpest new regulatory pressure (CBN pilot, SEC directive, EFCC crypto scrutiny).
- KYC/identity fraud (BVN/NIN black market) makes onboarding-fraud and insider-risk awareness a credible beginner service.
