# Win Nigerian clients by teaching beginner AI

A MERL or international-development consultant does best in Nigeria by avoiding the big tech stages. The better targets are rooms where evaluators, NGO managers, civil servants and professional-body members are being told to "get up to speed on AI" and have nobody to teach them. The strongest openings are these. The **Nigeria Evaluation Conference (2–4 Nov 2026, Abuja)**: attend it now and submit to the 2027 edition. **UNESCO/EU civil-service AI literacy workshops**, which are already running federally and in Enugu and Cross River. **NNNGO's facilitator channel and annual NGO conference**. A self-hosted Nigeria session in **gLOCAL Evaluation Week (early June 2027)**. And the **CPD programmes of NIM, CIPM and ICAN**, which already run AI capacity-building. Government and hub channels such as 3MTT, EkoAI Academy and CcHUB GATEWAY pay for *delivery partners and master trainers*, not one-off keynotes, so the best pitch to them is "facilitator plus M&E partner". Almost every late-2026 speaker call has closed. The real work is the 2027 cycle, and its pitch windows sit 3–9 months before each event: Q1 2027 for June events, Q2 for September–October events, and Q3 for November events. Two caveats apply to everything below. First, the research team could not open many official sites from its sandbox, so many details rest on search snippets and LinkedIn posts. Second, **no contact email in this report was verified**. Every route is an organisational website, platform page or social handle that must be confirmed before outreach.

**The evaluation community is the home base.** NAE2026 is themed "Evaluation as a National Asset" and includes a strand on **emerging trends and innovation**, which is a natural slot for AI in M&E. The abstract call circulated by LinkedIn repost and email, and the research team found no official NAE webpage, so the event details are unverified ([LinkedIn](https://ng.linkedin.com/in/seemberishuh)). NAE is described as the umbrella body for Nigeria's M&E associations ([FMINO](https://fmino.gov.ng/monitoring-evaluation-practitioners-get-professional-association/)). With the conference under two weeks away, the realistic move is to register, network with government M&E units and donor-project staff, and ask organizers in person how the 2027 abstract call will be circulated. gLOCAL is the lowest-barrier complement. Its 2026 edition was themed **"Evaluation, Evidence, and Trust in the Age of AI"**, sessions are volunteer-organized and free to attend ([BetterEvaluation](https://www.betterevaluation.org/community/events/glocal-evaluation-week-2026); [GEI](https://www.globalevaluationinitiative.org/glocal-home)), and Anglophone Africa organized **40 events** in 2024, Nigeria among them ([gLOCAL](https://glocalevalweek.org/node/64214)). A consultant can host a gLOCAL session without waiting for anyone's selection committee.

**Civil-service AI literacy is the closest match to a MERL profile.** In May 2026, OHCSF, UNESCO and the EU ran a three-day AI literacy workshop for senior federal civil servants and launched "Service-Wise GPT". The NITDA Director-General urged a **train-the-trainer approach** ([UNESCO](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/advancing-nigerias-digital-transformation-unesco-trains-civil-service-ai-and-digital-government); [Vanguard](https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/05/unesco-fg-train-senior-civil-servants-on-ethical-use-of-artificial-intelligence/)). Enugu has since trained permanent secretaries and directors with EU funding ([Gazette NGR](https://gazettengr.com/enugu-trains-permanent-secretaries-directors-on-ai-skills/)), and Cross River signed on for ethics, data-privacy and hands-on modules ([Cross River State](https://news.crossriverstate.gov.ng/criver-partners-unesco-eu-to-train-civil-servants-on-ai-literacy/)). The audience is beginners, the money comes from donors, and the rollout is spreading state by state. The entry point is UNESCO's Abuja office (X: @unesco_abuja) ([UNESCO Abuja](https://x.com/unesco_abuja/status/2052758899451646270)), and through it each state's Office of the Head of Service.

**NNNGO is the most direct NGO-capacity route, but it needs checking first.** NNNGO held its 2025 annual conference in Lagos on 12 November ([Forus](https://www.forus-international.org/fr/event/annual-conference-2025-nigeria-network-of-ngos)). It runs Google.org-supported digital-skills train-the-trainer workshops ([Nigeria Startup Act](https://www.nigeriastartupact.ng/call-for-applications-nnngo-digital-rights-and-cyber-safety-training/)) and has a [Call for Facilitators page](https://nnngo.org/call-for-facilitators/) that the team could not load. No 2026 conference announcement was found, so a November 2026 edition is inferred, not confirmed.

## Professional bodies and SME platforms pay for AI upskilling

Outside the development sector, the professional institutes are the most promising paid market. Their leaders are publicly pushing AI, and their CPD machinery needs facilitators. The **NIM president says the institute runs capacity-building "at zonal and chapter levels" on the implications of AI**, and NIM publishes an instructor-led virtual course calendar ([BusinessDay](https://businessday.ng/?p=1102979); [NIM](https://nim.ng/?p=1430)). ICAN's president says accountants will adopt AI tools while keeping professional ethics ([Leadership](https://leadership.ng/accountants-will-adopt-ai-tools-but-keep-professional-ethics-ican-president/)). ANAN runs fee-based zonal MCPD sessions, and its October 2026 Yola edition drew **2,000+ participants** ([NAN](https://nannews.ng/?p=343807)). CIPM bills its September conference as Africa's largest HR event, with workforce-transformation and digitalisation themes ([CIPM](https://cipmnigeria.org/cipm-announces-theme-dates-and-opens-registration-for-the-58th-international-conference-and-exhibition-africas-largest-hr-event/)). The 2026 flagship conferences of all these bodies have passed. The NBA's August conference, for example, had **27 breakout tracks and 200+ speakers** run by a planning committee ([NBA programme](https://blog.nigerianbar.org.ng/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/AGC2026_Beyond_Limits_Programme-1.pdf)). The accessible door is therefore the chapter and district level, where a beginner "AI for managers/accountants/HR" module is an easy proposal. None of these bodies publishes a formal speaker call. Selection by committee or invitation is inferred, and facilitator fees and CPD-accreditation rules were not found.

**For the business-owner audience, the gatekeeper is EDC Pan-Atlantic University.** It co-runs the WIMBIZ conference (5–6 Nov 2026, Eko Hotels, Lagos, which includes one-on-one "Fix Room" consultations) ([BellaNaija](https://www.bellanaija.com/?p=1787858); [WIMBIZ](https://ng.linkedin.com/in/wimbiz-ng)). It also delivers the training track for the Providus Bank SME Program and ran an agri-SME bootcamp with SMEDAN and AGRA ([TechEconomy](https://techeconomy.ng/edc-partners-empower-agric-smes-with-training-n1m-grants)). Access Bank's W Initiative mini-MBA for 120 women business owners, run with the IFC, starts in November 2026 ([MSME Africa](https://msmeafricaonline.com/access-bank-launches-8th-womenpreneur-programme-to-train-120-women-support-business-growth/)). These are prospecting-rich because the IFC, banks and SMEDAN sit behind them. NECA's summit becomes an **International Employers' Summit from 2027** ([VON](https://von.gov.ng/neca-upgrades-employers-summit-to-international-event/)). Its 2026 edition ran in late June ([ThisDay](https://thisdaylive.com/2026/06/25/neca-to-lead-conversation-on-impact-of-economic-reforms)), which points to roughly June 2027 for the next one. Its employer and HR audience buys AI upskilling.

## Government and hub programmes buy delivery partners, not keynotes

The research team found **no open 2026 call for external AI trainers from NITDA, NCAIR or 3MTT**. The live channels are partner and cascade models. That suits a consultant who can offer both facilitation and the learning-outcome monitoring these cascades visibly lack. 3MTT's state Learning Community Managers, one per state plus the FCT and funded by IHS Nigeria, **source volunteer facilitators, experts and venues for meetups**. That is the fastest nationwide route to unpaid but visible stage time ([FMCIDE](https://fmcide.gov.ng/articles/federal-ministry-of-communications-innovation-digital-economy-launches-3mtt-learning-community-in-partnership-with-ihs-nigeria/)). The [3MTT Partner Network](https://3mttpartnernetwork.com/) lists "training organisations and delivery partners" as a category, but whether it is official and whether it is accepting applications are both unverified. NITDA asked hubs, NGOs and institutions to register on its National Digital Literacy Dashboard, unveiled 20 Oct 2026 ([NITDA on X](https://x.com/NITDANigeria/status/2104926453972275610)). This is a visibility listing, not a speaking slot.

Master-trainer schemes recur and fit a "train the trainers, then evaluate the cascade" offer. Lagos's **EkoAI Academy** aims to reach 10,000 young people through 100 master trainers, and its applications were closed at the time of research ([Africa.com](https://africa.com/lagos-ai-programme-sets-out-to-build-the-pipeline-african-builders-have-been-missing/); [Lagos Innovates](https://talents.lagosinnovates.ng/)). The ECOWAS Small Business Coalition is training 250 lead trainers for 12,500 small businesses ([ITWeb Africa](https://itweb.africa/article/one-million-nigerians-set-to-dive-into-ai-training/mQwkoM6YalO73r9A)). **CcHUB's GATEWAY**, funded by the Mastercard Foundation, will train 340,000 young people in AI-enhanced digital skills through partner hubs in 10 states ([TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2026/03/23/cchub-gateway-program-to-train-340000-nigerians/)). Microsoft's Phase 2 skilling uses TeKnowledge to deliver training to 10,000 people, including a **"decision makers" track** that a beginner-AI facilitator could support as a sub-contractor ([TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2026/03/10/teknowledge-expands-partnership-with-microsoft/)). Tech4Dev has a development orientation and publishes research on Nigerians' use of generative AI ([Tech4Dev](https://tech4dev.com/resources/research)), so it is a natural guest-session and evaluation partner. In academia, **Nile University made AI literacy compulsory for all students and staff** ([Nile University](https://nileuniversity.edu.ng/future-of-artificial-intelligence/)), which creates staff-development demand. Lagos Business School sells a ₦1.2m executive AI programme, but it is faculty-led, so the way in is a centre-level partnership ([LBS](https://www.lbs.edu.ng/executive-education/open-seminar-prog/transformational-leadership-in-artificial-intelligence/)).

The tech-event circuit is easy to get onto and weak for prospecting. GDG DevFests are the only consistently public call-for-speakers channel. They use Sessionize, speaker tickets are waived (Abeokuta), and travel is self-funded with no stipend (Warri) ([Sessionize Abeokuta](https://sessionize.com/devfest-abeokuta-2026); [Sessionize Warri](https://sessionize.com/devfest-warri-2026/)). Their audiences are developers and students. The exceptions are the Abuja policy events, where development partners and ministries cluster: Nigeria AI Collective's **Build** (21–22 Oct 2026, with Governance & Ethics and Tech Justice tracks; abstract call closed) ([TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2026/09/30/nigeria-ai-collective-set-to-host-africas-first-shared-compute-and-ai-infrastructure-conference-build-2026/)) and the AI Safety Summit (4–5 Nov 2026, sourced only to a registration form, unverified) ([Tally](https://tally.so/r/ODMg98)). Big expos such as GITEX Nigeria, whose Abuja day was invitation-only ([AU-Startups](https://au-startups.com/events/gitex-nigeria-2026)), NES#32 ([NESG](https://www.nesgroup.org/32/agenda)) and the Lagos Trade Fair ([Guardian](https://guardian.ng/?p=2925686)) show no public calls for speakers. Use them for networking only.

## Outreach works best as one niche, three offers and verified routes

**Pitch a specialism, not "AI basics".** Generic AI-for-beginners content competes with Microsoft, Meta and free hub programmes ([Legit.ng](https://www.legit.ng/business-economy/technology/1725847-meta-launches-ai-academy-nigeria-participants-access-free-ai-training-5000-ad-credits/)). "Practical AI for programme, evidence and M&E teams" has almost no competition and leads straight to MERL engagements. Build three standard offers. The first is a free 60–90-minute beginner session for visibility venues: gLOCAL, 3MTT meetups, GDG chapters, and youth evaluation networks such as YEE Nigeria and NGYouthSDGs, which already run M&E trainings ([NGYouthSDGs](https://nigerianyouthsdgs.org/ngyouthsdgs-organizes-2-days-monitoring-and-evaluation-training-for-champions-across-nigeria/)). The second is a paid half-day or full-day workshop for CPD bodies, EDC Pan-Atlantic and NGO networks. The third is a "facilitate plus evaluate" package for cascade programmes like EkoAI, GATEWAY and UNESCO's civil-service rollout. That third offer is the one most likely to become a consulting contract. Have a Sessionize profile and two or three abstracts ready by May 2027, including one non-coder track and one hands-on prompting workshop. Write those abstracts yourself: PyCon Nigeria explicitly rejects AI-generated proposals ([PyCon Nigeria](https://ng.pycon.org/speaking/overview)), and other reviewers may do the same.

## Conclusion

Nigeria has a clear and growing gap here. Federal and state institutions, professional institutes and donor-funded skilling programmes have all committed publicly to AI literacy, but the visible supply is either developer-focused (DevFests, DSN) or corporate-owned (Microsoft, Meta). Almost nobody is teaching AI to the evaluators, programme managers and civil servants who will have to use it responsibly. A MERL consultant's advantage is credibility with exactly those audiences, plus the ability to measure whether cascaded AI training actually works. That second skill is what turns a speaking slot into a contract.

The practical implication is to treat 2027 as a sequence, not a scattershot. Use November 2026 (NAE, WIMBIZ, Startup Abuja) to meet organizers in person and confirm unverified routes. Lock in Q1 deals with CPD bodies and UNESCO-linked civil-service programmes while their annual plans are still open. Then run free sessions at gLOCAL and 3MTT meetups mid-year to build a track record and testimonials before the autumn conference season. Abuja deserves the most weight, because that is where development partners, ministries and the evaluation association converge.
